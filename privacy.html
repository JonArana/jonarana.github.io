Privacy Policy

Superstarry Poppin Mix is published by Star2nHero Studios. Last updated: November 2026.

1. Summary

Your game progress stays on your device. We do not run accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contacts. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect certain device and usage data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

2. Data stored on your device

Scores, Daily Mix results, achievements, settings, and saved runs are stored only on your device. We cannot see this data. Deleting the app, or using Clear Save Data in Settings, removes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner, full-screen, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • information about ads you see and interact with, and app usage;
  • technical and diagnostic data, such as crash and performance data.

This data is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and, where you allow it, show ads that fit your interests. Google's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Your choices

  • Apple devices: the first time you open the app, iOS asks whether to allow tracking. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or by resetting your advertising identifier in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: you will see a consent form from Google before personalised ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Mute: the Mute button turns off game sound and tells ads to play silently. It does not turn off ads.

If you do not allow tracking or consent, the game still works and you may still see ads that are not personalised.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it is not marked as made for kids. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for ages 13 and up.

6. Data retention and security

We do not operate servers that receive your game data. Google retains ad data under its own policies.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, where needed, the app.

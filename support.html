Common questions

Where is my save data?
Your progress is stored only on your device. It is not synced to any account or server.

How do I turn off sound or ads-related choices?
Use the Mute button on the main menu or pause screen. To change your ad consent or tracking choice, use Settings → Privacy Choices in the app (EEA/UK players), or change the tracking setting in iOS Settings.

How do I reset my game?
Use Clear Save Data in Settings. This removes your saved progress on the device.

I saw an ad during Continue. Can I skip it?
Continue and Resume are free if an ad is not ready or you are offline. Otherwise you can choose START A NEW RUN instead of watching the ad.
